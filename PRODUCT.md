# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A mixed audience; no single group is primary:

- **Data, ML and product leaders at mid-size and enterprise companies** who need production AI systems built and run (the clients behind the location-data and mapping case studies).
- **Small-business owners and founders** exploring AI for the first time, who need guidance as well as delivery.
- **Startups** that need senior AI/ML engineering capacity quickly.

In every case the visitor is evaluating whether to trust ASAP.ai TECHLABS with real work. Their job on the site: judge credibility, understand what the company does, and get in touch.

## Product Purpose

ASAP.ai TECHLABS is an AI consultancy based in Pune, India. It plans, builds and runs AI systems across six service lines: AI strategy and consulting, machine-learning solutions, generative AI development, data analytics and intelligence, process automation, and custom AI integration.

The website is the company's front door. Success means qualified visitors start a conversation: a free 30-minute consultation, an AI readiness assessment, or a direct inquiry by form, email or phone.

## Positioning

- **Speed to production.** The name stands for "As Soon As Possible": working AI shipped fast, without cutting quality.
- **Founder-led, senior work.** Clients work directly with an experienced principal, not a junior bench.
- **End-to-end ownership.** From strategy through build, deployment, monitoring and MLOps, not prototypes handed over.

## Operating Context

- Visitors reach the site through the LinkedIn company page, Instagram, printed business cards and direct referrals.
- Contact paths: a form that opens the visitor's email client addressed to contact@asapaitechlabs.com, phone +91 9822476698, and the Pune office address.
- Two named entry offers: a free 30-minute strategy consultation and an AI readiness assessment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **One page.** The site must stay a single scrolling page with anchor-link navigation; it must not be split into multiple pages or routes.
- **Static hosting.** A single `index.html` with assets in `assets/`, served by GitHub Pages at `asapaitechlabs.com` (`CNAME`). No build step, no backend.
- **No form backend.** The contact form composes a `mailto:` message; it does not submit or store data anywhere.
- **Terminology.** The company name is written exactly "ASAP.ai TECHLABS".

## Brand Commitments

- **The logo never changes.** The prism/hexagon mark (`assets/logo-mark.webp`), the wordmark (`assets/logo-wordmark.webp`) and the "ASAP.ai TECHLABS" lockup used in the nav and footer appear on the founder's business cards and all business communications, so their artwork, colors and wordmark styling must stay identical. Layout, palette and motion around them may change.
- **Company voice, anonymous founder.** The founder is presented as "AI Innovation Leader, Founder & Principal AI Consultant", writing as "we". Do not name an individual on the site.
- **Social presence.** LinkedIn (`linkedin.com/company/asap-ai-techlabs`) and Instagram (`instagram.com/asap.aitechlabs`).

## Evidence on Hand

**Can be used:**

- Three case studies, in `index.html` under "Our Work":
  1. Automated AI quality checks for a global location-data company (client project; client unnamed). XLM-RoBERTa, PyTorch, Hugging Face, Streamlit, AWS.
  2. An AI agent that keeps business listings up to date for a mapping company (client project; client unnamed). AI agents, Python, Streamlit, AWS S3.
  3. An in-house AI platform for contract review, built on 500+ real agreements. RAG, ChromaDB, fine-tuning, MLflow, Docker.
- Industry experience across healthcare, finance, retail, manufacturing and technology (confirmed).
- Over a decade of experience in AI and machine learning (confirmed).

**Do not claim** (not currently backed up):

- SOC 2 compliance or GDPR adherence.
- Official or strategic partnerships with OpenAI, Google Cloud AI, Azure AI or AWS. The company may say it *works with* these platforms, but not that it is a partner.

**Absent, and must not be invented:** client names or logos, testimonials, quantified results beyond what the case studies state, pricing, team size, certifications and awards.

## Product Principles

1. **Prove with real work.** Credibility comes from the case studies and plain statements of experience, never from claims the company can't back up.
2. **Speed is the promise.** The path from interest to conversation stays short and obvious, true to the name.
3. **Senior and accountable.** Speak with a principal's confidence, in plain language a first-time AI buyer understands, with technical depth available for specialist readers.
4. **Production over demos.** Show systems that run and are monitored in the real world, not just prototypes.
5. **One identity everywhere.** The site must stay consistent with the logo and materials clients already hold.
