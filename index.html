Multilingual ML Quality Monitoring for Place Categorization

A global location-data provider needed to know, before its customers did, whether the categories assigned to millions of places were still accurate. We built an automated pipeline that uses fine-tuned language models to audit those labels across regions and track quality over time.

  • Scheduled audits run monthly or quarterly across five regional models (US, APAC, Arabic, Spanish and Portuguese), scoring agreement with existing labels.
  • Disagreement analysis separates genuine model errors from errors in the source data, with a manual review interface to confirm findings.
  • Trend tracking and alerts monitor accuracy quarter over quarter, and any dip triggers an email investigation report.
  • Event-driven AWS deployment: a file upload triggers GPU inference on Spot instances, keeping idle costs to a few dollars a month.

Client projectMapping · Agentic AI

Agentic Integration Agent for Points-of-Interest Data Maintenance

A mapping and location-intelligence company was struggling to keep a multi-million-place database fresh: closed businesses still showing as open, missing contact details, and conflicting supplier data. We built the Integration Agent at the heart of their multi-agent maintenance framework.

  • PAVER workflow (Plan, Act, Validate, Execute, Re-evaluate, Share) gives every update a structured, auditable path.
  • Picks up candidates from an upstream Query Agent and enriches them through an AI web-verification service.
  • Smart comparison of current and enriched data: open/closed status, address and contact details, and category matching across two different taxonomies.
  • Review dashboard and full audit trail, with every request, response and decision logged per batch in cloud storage.

In-house platformLegal & compliance · GenAI

ComplianceAI Suite: Legal & Compliance Intelligence Platform

Our own enterprise GenAI platform for contract review and compliance Q&A, built on 500+ public commercial contracts annotated across 41 legal clause types. It is the reference architecture we bring to legal and compliance engagements.

  • Grounded RAG with citations: every answer is traced back to the exact contract passage it came from.
  • Hybrid retrieval fuses keyword and semantic search, then a cross-encoder re-ranks results for precision.
  • Multi-provider by design: swap between Anthropic, OpenAI and AWS Bedrock models and embeddings without code changes.
  • Domain fine-tuning of an open-source LLM with QLoRA for clause classification, with experiment tracking and automated quality evaluation.
