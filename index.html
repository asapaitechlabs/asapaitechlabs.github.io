Automated AI Quality Checks for Global Location Data

A global location-data company needed to spot wrong place categories before its customers did. We built an AI system that checks them automatically.

  • Automatic checks every month or quarter, across five markets.
  • Finds the real cause of each mismatch: a model mistake or bad source data.
  • Early alerts when quality drops, at a very low cloud cost.

02/03

AI Agent That Keeps Business Listings Up to Date

A mapping company had millions of listings going stale: closed shops shown as open, missing details and conflicting sources. We built an AI agent that finds and fixes them.

  • Checks every change step by step before it goes live.
  • Verifies status, address and category against live web data.
  • Keeps a full record of every decision, with a review dashboard.
